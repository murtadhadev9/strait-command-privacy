# Privacy Policy for Strait Command: Blockade

Last updated: 2026-10-15

Strait Studio ("we", "us") develops Strait Command: Blockade ("the app"). This policy explains what information the app handles.

## Information we collect

The app does not collect personal information. It does not require an account, and it does not send data to our servers or to third parties.

The app stores the following data only on your device:

- Player name (up to 24 characters), entered by you in the game
- Game progress, including waves cleared, best wave, campaign checkpoint and owned or equipped cosmetic colors
- Settings, including volume and graphics options

This data is saved using the device's local storage (Unity PlayerPrefs and the game's checkpoint file). It is not uploaded anywhere. Uninstalling the app or clearing its app data removes it.

## Information we do not collect

The app does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, crash-reporting services, online accounts, cloud saves or payment processing. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos.

## Children

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third-party services

The app does not integrate third-party services that collect data. Google Play may collect information about installs and purchases under its own privacy policy.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date will change when we do. Continued use of the app means you accept the updated policy.
